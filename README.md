# Week 1 — Strategic Research & Planning in Agriculture Data Analysis

## 📌 Overview

Week 1 established the foundation for the six-week Agribusiness Analytics project. The primary focus was to understand the role of data analytics in agriculture, identify relevant agricultural data sources, define analytical objectives, and develop a structured research and implementation plan.

The week focused on understanding how agricultural data can support better decision-making across crop production, weather analysis, agricultural markets, productivity and agribusiness operations.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Define the scope of agricultural data analytics.
* Identify relevant public agricultural datasets.
* Understand important agricultural data categories.
* Identify potential agribusiness analytical use cases.
* Define suitable analytical methodologies.
* Develop a structured six-week implementation roadmap.
* Identify project risks, limitations and resource requirements.

## 🔍 Key Activities

* Researched agricultural data platforms and public datasets.
* Studied crop production and productivity data.
* Researched rainfall and weather-related agricultural information.
* Investigated agricultural market and e-market data.
* Identified potential stakeholders and business use cases.
* Defined data requirements for future analysis.
* Developed a project timeline and milestone structure.
* Documented risks and mitigation approaches.
