# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Formula / definition | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Production | Reported production volume | Crop output |
| Area | Reported cultivated area | Scale |
| Yield | Production / area where compatible | Productivity |
| YoY growth | (Current - prior) / prior × 100 | Annual change |
| CAGR | (Ending / Beginning)^(1/n) - 1 | Long-term trend |
| Modal price | Source-reported modal market price | Market level |
| Price volatility | SD or CV over selected period | Market instability |
| Arrival quantity | Source-reported market arrivals | Supply flow |
| Traded quantity | Source-reported quantity traded | Market activity |
| Price spread | Max - min | Dispersion |
| Concentration | Top-state share or HHI where appropriate | Geographic concentration |
| Agriculture value added | World Bank/official indicator | Macro context |
