# Week 1 Source Research Notes

## Government of India — ES&E/DES
Primary source family for Indian agricultural statistics. The official site provides Agriculture Statistics at a Glance, current crop production estimates, MSP-related material and land-use statistics.

## DES Area, Production & Yield
The reporting interface supports State, District, Crops, Season and year selection, making it central to crop performance analysis.

## data.gov.in
The agriculture sector contains a large public catalogue and APIs. The district-wise, season-wise crop production resource provides crop, district, season and year dimensions with area and production measures. Coverage/update dates must be checked before treating a dataset as current.

## e-NAM
Official market information includes state, APMC, commodity, min/modal/max prices, arrivals and traded quantities, including historical trade data.

## FAOSTAT
Useful for international agricultural production, producer-price and production-value benchmarking.

## World Bank
Agriculture & Rural Development indicators provide macro context such as agriculture value added, land and yield indicators.

## ICAR
Annual reports, publications and coordinated crop research information provide agricultural research and technology context.

## MoSPI/NSS
Agricultural household and NSS reports provide socio-economic context, farming practices, assets, insurance and technology-related information.

## Evidence rule
Current claims should use current official sources. Historical datasets may be used for trend analysis only when their coverage period is clearly stated. Do not combine sources until units, definitions, geography and time periods have been reconciled.
